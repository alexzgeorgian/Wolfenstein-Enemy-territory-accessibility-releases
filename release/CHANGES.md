**Messages the server shows in the middle of the screen are now said - among them why a team would not take you - and Space on a class goes to its weapons.**

- When the server refuses something, or tells you something, with a line
  in the middle of the screen, the mod now says it. Joining a team that
  already has more players than the other is one: *"The Axis has too
  many players."* You stay a spectator until you choose the other team,
  or the teams even out.
- **Why Axis would not take you on the Mac**: hosting with bots, Axis
  had one player more, and with **Auto-Configure Server** on **Public
  Settings** the teams are kept even - the preset turns that on whatever
  **Balanced Teams** says. To join the bigger team anyway, set
  Auto-Configure Server to **No** and Balanced Teams **Off** before
  starting the server.

- **Space on a class goes to its weapons** in the limbo menu (L), as
  holding a mouse button on a class did: on the Class stop, or on a
  class in its open list, Space takes the class and puts you on its
  **Primary weapon** - Down is the secondary, Enter opens a list. A slot
  with only one weapon is now said too, "1 of 1", instead of being
  skipped.
