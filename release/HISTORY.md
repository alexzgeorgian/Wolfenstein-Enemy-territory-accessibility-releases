# Release history

Every version of this mod, newest first, going back to the first one
published. Each version is a heading, so a screen reader's heading keys
walk straight down the list without reading the entries in between:
jump back until you reach the version you were last running, and stop.

The build you have installed is described above this, under "This
build".


## 1.4.4.8 - 26 November 2026

**The host page and the in-match server info read out properly, and the campaign list said how to pick one map.**

- **Host page**: the checkboxes around Password said their names and
  whether they were on, and the list of maps or campaigns said which
  list it was and read each row as Up and Down moved through it.
- **Choosing one map**: with Game Type on campaigns, the list said
  *"Campaign list"* and *"For one map, set Game Type to Single-Map
  Objective"*.
- **Server info during a match** read the players row by row: name,
  score and ping.

## 1.4.4.7 - 23 November 2026

**N turned you even while you ran, turning no longer slowed you down, and the old objective report was quiet while a marker was out.**

- **N** turned you to face the way, then said *"Facing the way"*, even
  while you were running, and turned again by itself if the turn fell
  short.
- **Turning kept you running.** N's turn and the compass steps of the
  turn keys no longer held the walk key, so they no longer slowed you
  to a walk.
- **With a marker out**, getting stuck against a wall no longer read out
  the objective's straight line. Without a marker, it still did.

  flooded caves, to the Old City Water Pump.

## beta-0.00.5 - 24 September 2026

**Turning, looking, hearing the narrator over the music - and a settings
page for all of it.**

- The arrow keys turn and look. Stock ET binds no key to either, so the
  compass could name a direction and leave you no way to act on it.
- Up and down say what you end up pointing at, traced rather than
  described - *"Open sky"*, *"Concrete ground, 3 meters"*, *"Wooden
  ceiling"*.
- The map survey moved to the keypad's arrow cluster to make room, with
  Keypad 5 for "put the cursor back on me". That costs five of
  `selectbuddy`'s eight fireteam slots, taken deliberately.
- The music steps back while the narrator talks and comes straight back
  - the game's own music fade. Off by default; `eta_musicDuck 0.3` turns
  it on. A map that fades its own music keeps its level.
- Rate, pitch and volume for SAPI 5. Not for NVDA, deliberately: NVDA
  speaks at the settings its own user chose.
- Settings take effect when you change them. Every setting the mod adds
  was read once at map load and never re-read, so changing one stored
  the value and changed nothing until the next map.
- An Accessibility page in the Options menu, holding all of it.
- No `centerview` key, because ET: Legacy has no such command - the one
  its own left-handed config binds to HOME is registered nowhere and has
  been dead for years.

## beta-0.00.3 - 23 September 2026

**Sliders step with the arrow keys - the last control that needed a

- Left and right step a slider and the new value is spoken: effects
  volume, music volume, mouse sensitivity, and the forty-odd others.
- Round numbers rather than a flat twentieth of each range, because
  the ranges are nothing alike - 0 to 1 for a volume, 1 to 30 for

  the first press, and pressing past an end says the value again
  rather than going silent.
- Dragging with a mouse is untouched.

## beta-0.00.2 - 23 September 2026

**The build says when it is done, NVDA works out of the box, and Enter
changes the setting you are on.**

- The construction narration finishes properly: *"Building, 91
  percent"*, then *"Construction complete"*. The previous release
  stopped at 75 and said nothing more, on my mistaken reading that the
  engine gave no honest completion signal. It does - finishing a task
  sends the client an explicit completed event, which is what fills the
  bar to the top on screen - and I had stopped reading one line short
  of it.
- The NVDA controller client is in the download now, both
  architectures with NV Access's own licence text, verified against
  checksums recorded in the repository. It used to be a separate errand
  that sent people searching the web for a DLL by name.
- Enter changes the setting you are on. Every settings control in the
  game demanded the mouse pointer be inside it as well as having
  keyboard focus, and arrowing onto one does not move the pointer - so
  Enter did nothing, on every checkbox, every yes/no and every game

  server. Sliders and key-binding rows are still mouse-only.

## alpha-0.19.1 - 22 September 2026

**Stairs, and what the icons under your crosshair mean.**

- Stairs are called ahead of you, up or down, and a single step is told
  from a flight of them. The ground is measured rather than looked at:
  flat ground a short way ahead at a height you could step to is a step,
  and if the ground beyond keeps going the same way it is a flight.
- A ramp is left unsaid rather than called stairs. A tread is flat and a
  slope is not, and sending someone to feel for steps that are not
  there, on a surface they might walk off, is worse than saying nothing.
- What you can use is named when the game shows the icon for it -
  "pliers, build here", "pliers, defuse here", "dynamite goes here",
  "revive", "health pack" - with the progress figure where the game
  draws a bar beside the icon. The interaction sound still fires the
  instant the icon appears; the words follow a quarter of a second
  behind, so turning through a supply room does not read out everything
  you swept past.
- The reported "wall, 0 metres" while walking was addressed: the probe
  became a sweep of the player's own body rather than a line from the
  eye, and the scenery was moved ahead of the objective callout. Both
  were right and neither was enough - see alpha-0.20.0.

## alpha-0.19.0 - 22 September 2026

**Doors creak, the crosshair ticks, and the map tells you where you are.**

- Every door within about ten metres has a creaking sound placed on it,
  positioned in the world, so it swings around you as you turn and
  centres when you face it. A sound that is still there cannot be missed
  the way a spoken line can. A locked door creaks duller and slower, so
  you can hear that it will not open without walking to it.
- Doors are identified by the game's own label, not guessed from the
  entity, so lifts, tank barriers and movable bridges stay silent. I had
  told the tester this would not be possible; that was wrong, and being
  wrong made the feature better.
- The crosshair ticks when it is on somebody - bright for an enemy, low
  and soft for a teammate, six times a second so a quick sweep still
  registers. It reads the game's own crosshair, so it never sees through
  a wall, and a disguised covert operative sounds like a teammate,
  because that is what a sighted player sees.
- Says where you are in the map's own words - *"Old City Wall"*, *"Axis
  Command Post"* - from the same list that fills in the location when a
  sighted player types in team chat, with the command map's grid square
  as a fallback for maps that ship no list. The status key also reports
  whether you have open sky overhead.
- The first four sounds this mod brings with it, generated from readable
  source rather than shipped as opaque files, and packed into the pk3.
  The build refuses to make a release if any sound the mod asks for was
  not produced.
- Writing the check for the location narration found a real defect in
  it: a place name longer than the buffer was stored truncated but
  compared in full, so it never matched, restarted its timer every frame
  and could never be announced at all.

## alpha-0.18.0 - 22 September 2026

**Empty space you can fall into, and water you can swim in.**

- Says when the ground ahead falls away, how far down it goes, and
  whether the fall would hurt or kill you. Both of those bands are the
  game's own falling-damage thresholds, converted back into heights
  using the gravity actually in play on the server.
- Says how deep in water you are - wading, deep enough that a jump

- The compass no longer trails behind the mouse. It was not slow, it
  was behind: a sweep crossed four headings in a few frames and every
  one was announced. A heading now has to be held briefly before it is
  spoken, so a sweep says where you ended up rather than reading out
  everything it passed.
- Checked, after a report: switching weapons with the number keys still
  announces normally. Only the three scope toggles are silenced.
